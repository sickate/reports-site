## Findings
- Argan Inc. is a NYSE-listed holding company operating primarily as a construction firm through four wholly owned subsidiaries: Gemma Power Systems (GPS), Atlantic Projects Company (APC), The Roberts Company (TRC), and Southern Maryland Cable / SMC Infrastructure Solutions (SMC). [1][2]
- Argan reports three segments: Power Industry Services (GPS + APC), Industrial Construction Services (TRC, operating as "TRC"/TRO), and Telecommunications Infrastructure Services (SMC). [1][5]
- For FY2026 (year ended Jan 31, 2026), Argan reported full-year revenue of $944.6M and net income of $137.8M ($9.74 diluted EPS), with gross margin rising to 20.5%. [5][6]
- Q4 FY2026 segment mix: Power ~$204M (78% of total), Industrial ~$53M (20%), Teledata ~$5M (~2%), with Power generating ~$55M pre-tax income. [5]
- Consolidated project backlog grew to ~$2.9B at Jan 31, 2026 (up from ~$1.4B a year earlier), with ~$2.5B of new contract value added during FY2026. [5][6]
- Gemma Power Systems has built more than 16,000 MW of generating capacity across combined-cycle and simple-cycle gas plants, biomass, solar, wind, biofuel and environmental facilities, and is the primary earnings driver. [1][2]
- Major recent EPC wins: 1,350 MW combined-cycle plant in Texas (Full NTP Oct 2025), 1.2 GW Texas project (Feb 2025), 920 MW West Virginia gas project, and the 1.4 GW CPV Basin Ranch Energy Center plus an 860 MW Texas facility added in Q3 FY2026. [7][8]
- David H. Watson has served as President, CEO and Director since August 2022, succeeding founder Rainer Bosselmann; Watson previously served as Argan's SVP/CFO/Treasurer from October 2015 and is a CPA who was CFO of Gladstone Investment Corp (2010-2015). [3][4]
- The Roberts Company provides fully integrated industrial construction, fabrication (pressure vessels, heat exchangers, piping) and on-site plant/turnaround services to industrial clients in agriculture, petrochemical, pulp & paper, water treatment, data centers and power, primarily in the southern US. [2][5]
- SMC Infrastructure Solutions provides telecom/broadband and power-distribution infrastructure project management, construction and maintenance, mainly in the US Mid-Atlantic region and to federal-government customers. [1][2]
