Sundar Pichai· CEOSubscriptions / Gemini

Anat Ashkenazi· CFO回购暂停
"Our priority this quarter has been funding our compute infrastructure investments and the Wiz acquisition; we did not repurchase any shares in Q1."本季的资本配置优先级是支持算力基础设施投入和 Wiz 收购；Q1 我们没有回购任何股份。
